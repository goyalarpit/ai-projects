# Wanderlust — Hawa Mahal Puzzle

A geometric tile-snap puzzle game. Fill the arched windows of Hawa Mahal with shaped pieces to wake the palace at sunrise.

**Play it:** https://goyalarpit.github.io/ai-projects/wanderlust/

## How to Play
1. Read the wind's narration, then tap **Begin Puzzle**
2. Tap a **piece** in the tray to select it (gold outline appears)
3. Tap a **glowing slot** on the grid to place it
4. Tap a **placed piece** to lift it back to the tray
5. Fill all 5 windows — watch the palace light up at sunrise

## Puzzle Windows
| # | Window | Grid | Pieces |
|---|--------|------|--------|
| 1 | Side Gallery Window | 4×4 | 5 |
| 2 | Left Tower Arch | 4×5 | 6 |
| 3 | Central Royal Arch | 5×5 | 7 |
| 4 | Right Tower Arch | 4×5 | 6 |
| 5 | Grand Entrance | 4×6 | 7 |

## Tech
Pure HTML + CSS + JS. No dependencies. Works on mobile and desktop.
